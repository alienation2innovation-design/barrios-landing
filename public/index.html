Questions

What business owners usually ask first.

What can you automate?

Any repeatable computer-based workflow can be evaluated. We focus this service on marketing, lead handling, sales follow-up, customer communication, and the reporting around those activities.

Do I need to know exactly what to build?

No. Start with the result you want, the work that keeps repeating, or the place customers are getting stuck. We map the system from there.

Will I need to replace my current software?

Usually not. We first look for safe ways to connect and improve the tools your business already relies on.

Can people approve important steps?

Yes. Human review can remain anywhere a decision needs judgment, accountability, or personal attention.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the workflow, integrations, volume, and ongoing support required. We define the scope before recommending a build.
